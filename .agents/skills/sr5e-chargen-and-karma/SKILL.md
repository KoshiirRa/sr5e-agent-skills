# Shadowrun 5e Character Generation & Karma Progression

This skill covers the mechanics of Priority Character Creation, the Sum-to-Ten system, character generation constraints, and Karma advancement costs in Shadowrun 5th Edition.

---

## 1. Character Generation Limits & Constraints

- **Availability Cap**: Standard chargen limits all starting gear, weapons, cyberware, and vehicles to a maximum **Availability of 12** (and no Forbidden `F` gear, only Standard or Restricted `R`, unless permitted by GM or specific quality).
- **Attribute Limits**: Only **one** mental or physical attribute may be raised to the character's natural maximum at character creation.
- **Karma-to-Nuyen Conversion**: A character may convert up to **$10$ Karma** into additional starting nuyen at a rate of **$1\text{ Karma} = 2{,}000¥$** (up to $20{,}000¥$ extra).

---

## 2. Priority System & Sum-to-Ten

In SR5, characters are generated by assigning priority ranks **A, B, C, D, E** across five categories:

| Priority | Metatype (Special Attributes) | Attributes | Magic or Resonance | Skills | Resources (Nuyen) |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **A** | Human (9), Elf (8), Dwarf (7), Ork (7), Troll (5) | 24 | Mag/Res 6, two R5 skills, 10 spells/CFs | 46 / 10 | 450,000¥ |
| **B** | Human (7), Elf (6), Dwarf (4), Ork (4), Troll (0) | 20 | Mag/Res 4, two R4 skills, 7 spells/CFs | 36 / 5 | 275,000¥ |
| **C** | Human (5), Elf (3), Dwarf (1), Ork (0) | 16 | Mag/Res 3, 5 spells/CFs | 28 / 2 | 140,000¥ |
| **D** | Human (3), Elf (0) | 14 | Magician/Adept 2, or Aspected/Techno 2 | 22 / 0 | 50,000¥ |
| **E** | Human (1) | 12 | Mundane (None) | 18 / 0 | 6,000¥ |

### Sum-to-Ten Point Values:
Characters using the Sum-to-Ten variant assign points that sum exactly to **10**:
- **A** = 4 points
- **B** = 3 points
- **C** = 2 points
- **D** = 1 point
- **E** = 0 points

## 3. Karma Advancement & Progression Costs

Advancing a character during play or spending leftover character generation Karma follows these formulas:

| Advancement Option | Karma Cost Formula | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **New Active Skill (Rating 1)** | $2\text{ Karma}$ | First rank in Sneaking = 2 Karma |
| **Improve Active Skill** | $\text{New Rating} \times 2$ | Raising Skill from 3 to 4 = $8\text{ Karma}$ |
| **New Skill Group (Rating 1)** | $5\text{ Karma}$ | First rank in Athletics Group = 5 Karma |
| **Improve Skill Group** | $\text{New Rating} \times 5$ | Raising Group from 2 to 3 = $15\text{ Karma}$ |
| **Skill Specialization** | $7\text{ Karma}$ | Adding *Semi-Automatics* to Pistols = 7 Karma |
| **Improve Physical / Mental Attribute** | $\text{New Rating} \times 5$ | Raising Agility from 4 to 5 = $25\text{ Karma}$ |
| **Improve Special Attribute (Magic / Resonance)** | $\text{New Rating} \times 5$ | Raising Magic from 5 to 6 = $30\text{ Karma}$ |
| **New Knowledge or Language Skill (Rating 1)** | $1\text{ Karma}$ | First rank = 1 Karma |
| **Improve Knowledge or Language Skill** | $\text{New Rating} \times 1$ | Raising from 2 to 3 = $3\text{ Karma}$ |
| **Learn New Spell / Complex Form** | $5\text{ Karma}$ | Learning *Stunbolt* = 5 Karma |
| **Initiation / Submersion Grade** | $10 + (\text{Grade} \times 3)$ | Grade 1 = $13\text{ Karma}$; Grade 2 = $16\text{ Karma}$ |
| **Purchase Positive Quality (in play)** | $\text{Karma Cost} \times 2$ | 5-point quality = $10\text{ Karma}$ (requires GM approval) |
| **Remove Negative Quality (in play)** | $\text{Karma Bonus} \times 2$ | Buying off 10-point negative quality = $20\text{ Karma}$ |
